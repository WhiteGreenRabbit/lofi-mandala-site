Lofi Mandala

Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 12, 2026

Short version: Lofi Mandala stores your coloring progress and preferences on your device only. If you sign up for our mailing list, we collect your email address via Beehiiv. The app uses Sentry for crash reporting — if the app crashes, diagnostic data (device model, OS version, stack trace) is sent to Sentry. The app shows ads, which may use your device's advertising identifier if you grant permission when prompted. We never sell your data.

Information We Collect

The Lofi Mandala app does not collect any personal information. It has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no backend server.

If you sign up for our mailing list via lofimandala.com, we collect your email address for the purpose of sending you product updates, new mandala announcements, and launch notifications. We use Beehiiv to manage our mailing list. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link included in every email. We do not sell, share, or trade your email address with any third party. California residents may request deletion of their data by emailing forkedtogether@gmail.com.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app saves the following data locally on your device using your phone's built-in storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us:

  • Coloring progress — which regions you've filled and what colors you chose
  • Color palette — your saved custom colors
  • Settings — your preferred music track, canvas background, and sound preferences

You can delete all of this data at any time from the app's Settings screen using the "Delete all data" option.

Audio

All music tracks and sound effects in Lofi Mandala are bundled within the app. The app does not stream audio from the internet or connect to any music service.

Advertising

Lofi Mandala shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Ads are served to support free access to the app — we do not sell your personal data.

AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier to show relevant ads and measure their effectiveness:

  • iOS: IDFA (Identifier for Advertisers). You will be shown Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before any tracking occurs. If you decline, AdMob will show non-personalised ads instead. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: GAID (Google Advertising ID). You can opt out of personalised ads in Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID, or enable "Opt out of Ads Personalisation".

AdMob may collect approximate location (country/city level), device information, and app interaction data for ad targeting and measurement. This data is processed by Google under their privacy policy. We do not receive or store this data ourselves.

Third-Party Services

Lofi Mandala uses Google AdMob for in-app advertising. AdMob's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

Lofi Mandala uses Sentry for crash reporting. If the app crashes, Sentry automatically collects diagnostic data including device model, OS version, app version, and a crash stack trace. This data is used solely to identify and fix bugs. No personal information (name, email, IP address) is included. Sentry's privacy policy is available at sentry.io/privacy.

Our mailing list is managed by Beehiiv. When you subscribe, your email address is stored and processed by Beehiiv under their privacy policy. Beehiiv is GDPR and CAN-SPAM compliant and handles all unsubscribe requests automatically.

The Lofi Mandala website (lofimandala.com) uses Google Tag Manager and loads fonts from Google Fonts. These services may collect your IP address and set cookies for analytics and font delivery purposes. Google's privacy policy applies to data collected through these services.

Font data is requested from Google's servers when you visit the website. If you prefer, you can block these requests using a browser content blocker.

Children's Privacy

Lofi Mandala is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under the age of 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising is configured for general audiences and is not targeted at children.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will revise the "last updated" date above. Significant changes will be noted in the app update release notes.

Contact
