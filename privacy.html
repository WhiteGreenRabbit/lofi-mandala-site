Lofi Mandala

Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Short version: Lofi Mandala stores your coloring progress and preferences on your device only. The app shows ads, which may use your device's advertising identifier if you grant permission when prompted.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. Lofi Mandala has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no backend server.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app saves the following data locally on your device using your phone's built-in storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us:

  • Coloring progress — which regions you've filled and what colors you chose
  • Color palette — your saved custom colors
  • Settings — your preferred music track, canvas background, and sound preferences

You can delete all of this data at any time from the app's Settings screen using the "Delete all data" option.

Audio

All music tracks and sound effects in Lofi Mandala are bundled within the app. The app does not stream audio from the internet or connect to any music service.

Advertising

Lofi Mandala shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS) to show you relevant ads and measure their effectiveness.

On iOS, you will be shown Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before any tracking occurs. If you decline, AdMob will show non-personalised ads instead. You can change this permission at any time in your device Settings under Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Third-Party Services

Lofi Mandala uses Google AdMob for advertising. AdMob's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy. The app does not use any analytics, crash reporting, or other tracking services.

Children's Privacy

Lofi Mandala is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under the age of 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising is configured for general audiences and is not targeted at children.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will revise the "last updated" date above. Significant changes will be noted in the app update release notes.
