Getting Started

Tap any mandala from the gallery to open it. Choose a color from the palette at the bottom of the screen, then tap any region to fill it. There are no rules — color however feels right.

Pinch to zoom in on detailed areas. Use two fingers to pan around the canvas. Long-press and drag with one finger to pan at any zoom level.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I undo a fill?

Tap the undo button (↩) in the toolbar above the canvas to undo your last fill. You can undo multiple steps.

How do I erase a region?

Tap the eraser icon in the toolbar to switch to erase mode, then tap any filled region to clear it.

How do I add my own colors?

Long-press any of the empty custom color slots at the right end of the palette to open the color picker. Your custom colors are saved automatically.

How do I change the music?

Tap the music bar at the bottom of the gallery or paint screen to open the track picker. You can also control playback from there.

How do I reset a mandala?

Open the mandala, then tap the reset button in the top toolbar. This will clear all fills for that mandala.

Where is my progress saved?

All progress is saved automatically on your device. It does not require an internet connection and is not stored on any server.

How do I delete all my data?

Go to Settings (tap the icon in the top-right corner of the gallery) and scroll to the Data section. Tap "Delete all data" to wipe all progress and return the app to its default state.
