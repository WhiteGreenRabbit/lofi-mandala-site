About the Music

All music tracks featured in Lofi Mandala were created using Suno, an AI music generation platform. The tracks were composed specifically for use in this app to provide a calm, meditative coloring experience.

Commercial Licensing

White Green Rabbit, the development company behind Lofi Mandala, maintains an active paid subscription to Suno. Under Suno's Terms of Service, paid subscribers are assigned full ownership and commercial use rights over all music generated during their subscription period.

This means the music in Lofi Mandala is licensed for commercial use, including distribution within mobile applications on the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.

Ownership

The music tracks featured in Lofi Mandala are owned by White Green Rabbit under the rights assigned by Suno's paid subscription terms. They are not available for redistribution, resale, or use outside of this application without explicit written permission from White Green Rabbit.

Future Music

We hope to partner with independent lofi artists in the future, giving talented musicians a platform to reach new listeners. If you are an artist interested in being featured in Lofi Mandala, please get in touch via our support page.
